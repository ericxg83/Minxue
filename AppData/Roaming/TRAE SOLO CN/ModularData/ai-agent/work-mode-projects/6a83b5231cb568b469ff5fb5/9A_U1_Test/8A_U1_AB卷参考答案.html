解析要点： ① enough 用法：① adj./adv. + enough (clean enough 足够干净)；② enough + n. (enough money/water 足够的钱/水)；注意不能用于 too … enough 句式；
② more than = over (超过)：more than three billion / more than two metres / more than ten years 等；
③ How much / How many：How much 提问不可数名词 (water, money, pollution)，How many 提问可数名词复数 (factories, bottles, students, litres)；
④ take pride in = be proud of：主语为单数第三人称用 takes pride in，过去式用 took pride in；
⑤ Shall we do...?：用于提建议，后接动词原形；
⑥ the + 比较级 …, the + 比较级 …：表示"越……，就越……"，前后都用 the + 比较级 (The more …, the more / better / less / cleaner …)。
